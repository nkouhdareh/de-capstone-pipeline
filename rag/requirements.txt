# No sentence-segmentation library. pysbd was tried and removed: profiling showed
# it was 50% of total chunking runtime (988,976 regex compilations for 347 labels),
# and it did not know unit abbreviations like "mg." anyway. chunker.py masks those
# abbreviations itself and splits with one regex. 197s -> 30s on the same input.
